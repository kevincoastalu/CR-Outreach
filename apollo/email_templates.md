# Cold outbound sequence — privacy-focused law firms

ICP: attorneys/partners at law firms that specialize in privacy law (privacy
litigation, privacy compliance/counsel, data security practice groups).

Angle: CyRisk's Insight Engine scans any target website (a client, a
prospective client, a matter, an M&A target) and returns the specific
technical evidence behind today's privacy-tracking litigation — CIPA/wiretapping,
VPPA, CCPA/CPRA. We are **not** pre-scanning the recipient's own firm for this
outreach; the hook is the capability itself, plus an open offer to run a scan
on a target of their choosing and walk through the findings.
